# Cinematic UI Designer Portfolio - Comprehensive Design Prompt

## 🎬 Core Aesthetic Vision
Create an award-winning, Awwwards-style, highly cinematic UI designer portfolio. The design must completely abandon any "gamer" or "neon cyberpunk" aesthetics (no hyper-saturated pinks, no bouncing badges, no spinning cosmic rings, and no faux hacker terminals). Instead, it must embody the sheer elegance, maturity, and sophistication of top-tier tech companies like Vercel, Linear, and Apple. The goal is to look expensive, atmospheric, and profoundly polished.

## 🎨 Color Palette: "Obsidian Elegance & Ethereal Frost"
The color scheme is rooted in moody, deep monochromatic tones that mimic low-light cinematic photography, with light used only to draw the eye to critical interactive elements.

*   **Primary Background:** Deep Obsidian `#0A0A0A` (A rich, true dark canvas)
*   **Secondary Surface (Cards/Nav):** Midnight Charcoal `#121212` with a subtle frosted glass effect.
*   **Borders & Dividers:** `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)` (Extremely subtle, crisp 1px lines)
*   **Primary Text:** Brilliant White `#F8FAFC` (For massive, confident headings)
*   **Secondary Text:** Muted Slate `#94A3B8` (For readable, elegant body copy)
*   **The Cinematic Accent:** "Ethereal Frost" `rgba(241, 245, 249, 0.8)` or a very soft, sheer icy blue `#E2E8F0`. Used *only* for hover states, subtle button glows, and micro-interactions.

## 🏗️ Structural & Modern Elements
*   **Typography:** Massive, beautifully kerned Sans-Serif fonts (`Inter` or `Clash Display`). High contrast between giant display headings and perfectly legible body text.
*   **Negative Space:** Vast amounts of empty space. Content should breathe. Elements must not feel cramped or clustered.
*   **Glassmorphism Done Right:** Deep backdrop blurs (`blur(30px)`) with pure white, low-opacity borders (`rgba(255,255,255,0.06)`). No colored borders.
*   **Bento Grid Layouts:** Clean, structured, asymmetrical bento grids for projects and skills. Content inside is meticulously aligned.

## 🎭 Cinematic Animations & Smoothness
*   **Physics-Based Easing:** Every single transition must use premium bezier curves (e.g., `cubic-bezier(0.16, 1, 0.3, 1)`).
*   **Hover Effects:** Magnetic buttons, soft scaling (`scale(1.02)`), and deep, soft, colorless drop shadows (`0 20px 40px rgba(0,0,0,0.5)`).
*   **Scroll & Parallax:** Elements should fade in softly (`opacity: 0` to `1` with a gentle `translateY`) as the user scrolls down the page. No erratic bouncing or continuous spinning loops.
*   **Atmospheric Lighting:** Instead of bright neon orbs, use massive, extremely blurred (`blur(200px)`), low-opacity (`0.05`) white/slate radial gradients in the background to simulate soft studio lighting hitting the page.
